Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 17, 2025

Welcome to Aspira, located at https://aspira-roadmaps.vercel.app/. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and protect your information.

Information We Collect

Aspira may collect account information such as email addresses when users create an account to save or edit a roadmap. Quiz answers and generated roadmap data may also be stored so the dashboard can show your saved plan.

Account Features

Account features allow users to save, edit, and revisit roadmap progress. Login and authentication are handled through Firebase services.

Cookies and Local Storage

We may use cookies or local storage to remember quiz responses or preferences to improve your experience. Sensitive information should not be stored in local browser storage.

Google AdSense

Aspira may use Google AdSense to display ads. Google may collect anonymized data through cookies or similar technologies to serve more relevant ads. Learn more about how Google uses data at Google's advertising policies.

Third-Party Links

Our site may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at aspira.roadmaps@gmail.com.
